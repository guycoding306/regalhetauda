Helpful Information

Frequently Asked
Questions.

Here are a few helpful answers before you get in touch.

How can I enquire about a room reservation?

Visit our Reservations page to select your preferred dates and provide your details. Our team can then confirm availability and booking arrangements.

Can I enquire about dining arrangements?

Yes. Use the contact form and select Dining Enquiry to ask about current dining options, menus or group arrangements.

Can I discuss a special event or celebration?

You can contact the resort to discuss your plans and ask which arrangements may be available for your event.

Where is Regal Hetauda Resort located?

Regal Hetauda Resort is in Hetauda, Bagmati Province, Nepal. You can use the Google Map above for directions.
