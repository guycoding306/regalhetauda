Welcome to Regal

A Place Where
Memories Begin.

Located in Hetauda, Nepal, Regal Hetauda Resort welcomes guests looking for comfort, relaxation and a memorable hospitality experience.

Our vision is to create an inviting setting where guests can step away from their everyday routines and enjoy meaningful moments with family, friends or loved ones.

From the atmosphere of our accommodation to the care behind each guest interaction, we aim to make every visit feel comfortable, personal and memorable.
